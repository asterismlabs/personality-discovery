# Sources

Item wording for the two published instruments is copied, not rewritten. Original sections are labeled in the app.

## 1. Personality: IPIP-NEO-120

- Instrument: 120-item IPIP representation of the 30 NEO-PI-R facets (IPIP-NEO-120).
- Items and keying: [The Items in Each of the 4-Item IPIP Scales](https://ipip.ori.org/30FacetNEO-PI-RItems.htm).
- Permission: [IPIP permission statement](https://ipip.ori.org/newPermission.htm). The IPIP is in the public domain. No further permission is required.
- Citation: Johnson, J. A. (2014). Measuring thirty facets of the Five Factor Model with a 120-item public domain inventory: Development of the IPIP-NEO-120. *Journal of Research in Personality, 51*, 78–89. [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jrp.2014.05.003](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jrp.2014.05.003)
- Response scale: Very Inaccurate, Moderately Inaccurate, Neither Accurate Nor Inaccurate, Moderately Accurate, Very Accurate.
- Modifications: item order is shuffled within the section. Two directed reading-check items were added and are not scored. Wording and keying of the 120 items are unchanged. Domain scores are the mean of the answered items in that domain (24 items), not a normed T-score.

## 2. Vocational interests: O*NET Interest Profiler Short Form

- Instrument: O*NET Interest Profiler Short Form, paper-and-pencil, 60 activities.
- Source file: [Interest Profiler PDF](https://www.onetcenter.org/dl_tools/ipsf/Interest_Profiler.pdf), linked from [O*NET Interest Profiler](https://www.onetcenter.org/IP.html).
- License: [CC BY-ND 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nd/4.0/), as described on the [O*NET Career Exploration Tools license page](https://www.onetcenter.org/license_tools.html). This app uses the no-modification option.
- Attribution, also shown in the app: This application includes information from the [O*NET Career Exploration Tools](https://www.onetcenter.org/tools.html) by the U.S. Department of Labor, Employment and Training Administration (USDOL/ETA). Used under the CC BY-ND 4.0 license. O*NET® is a trademark of USDOL/ETA.
- The form also says: Sponsored by the U.S. Department of Labor, Employment & Training Administration. Developed by the National Center for O*NET Development.
- Response format: check the activities you would like to do. Score: count of checks in each area, 0–10.
- Item order: left to right, then top to bottom, of the two-column paper form. The screen shows that sequence in one column.
- Modifications to the licensed text: none. Items, order, checklist response, and 0–10 scoring are unchanged. No reading-check items are inserted in this section. USDOL/ETA has not endorsed this application.
- Separate from the licensed instrument: Low / Moderate / High labels and the ideas-versus-data and people-versus-things scores. Those use formulas noted below and are labeled in the app as this app's interpretation.

### Ideas / data and people / things

Computed by this app from percent-of-maximum interest scores (0–100), not from the official 0–10 display:

- Data versus ideas = 1.73 × (Enterprising + Conventional − Investigative − Artistic). Positive is toward data.
- People versus things = 2×Social + Artistic + Enterprising − 2×Realistic − Investigative − Conventional. Positive is toward people.

Reference for the dimensions: Prediger, D. J. (1982). Dimensions underlying Holland's hexagon: Missing link between interests and occupations? *Journal of Vocational Behavior, 21*, 259–287. The formulas are the ones implemented in this app's scoring code. A score within 5% of the largest possible swing is described as near the middle.

## 3. Work values

Original items written for this app, three per theme: achievement, independence, recognition, relationships, support, and working conditions. They are not O*NET Work Importance Locator or Work Importance Profiler items. Those tools are retired, and rewriting them onto a Likert scale would be a modification of licensed material. These items are labeled **unvalidated**.

## 4. Work style

Original items written for this app: tolerance for ambiguity, need for structure, preference for working with others, pace and feedback, and risk tolerance. Labeled **exploratory, unvalidated**. This is not an ability measure and not a published instrument.

## 5. Descriptions

The plain-language sentences next to scores were written for this app. They are not copied from IPIP or from O*NET materials.
